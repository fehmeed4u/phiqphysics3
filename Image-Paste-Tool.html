Image paste tool

Fill every lesson diagram slot by pasting screenshots, then download them all as one correctly-named zip. Send me the zip and I will drop the images into assets/lesson-images/.

  1. Click a slot to select it (the first empty one is selected automatically).
  2. Copy your screenshot, then press Ctrl+V / ⌘+V. You can also drag an image file onto a slot.
  3. Selection jumps to the next empty slot, so you can paste straight through a batch.
  4. Hit Download zip when done. Your work is saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back.
