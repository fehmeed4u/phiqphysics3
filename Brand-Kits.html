Brand kit reference · φQ Physics

Five brand kits
for the physics site

All five share one type system, one spacing rhythm and one structural vocabulary, so slides built in any of them read as the same product. The variation is in colour temperature and mood only.

Kit 01 is the site's current dark theme and Kit 02 its light counterpart, so either can be used without re-skinning anything. Kits 03–05 are drop-in alternatives on the same skeleton — useful for separating topics, or for a deck that needs to sit apart from the site without leaving the family.

Constant across all five kits

Do not vary these between kits — they are what makes the set read as one brand.

Using these with another tool

  • Pick one kit per deck and stay in it. Mixing two within a deck breaks the uniformity these are designed to give you.
  • Always pass the constant block alongside the kit's colour list — the type system and the left-rule equation/callout pattern are what carry the brand, not the hue.
  • Semantic roles are fixed across all five: accent = definition or note, warning = trap or common mistake, success = examiner tip or model answer, error = misconception only.
  • The existing lesson figures are black line art on white. Kits 02 and 05 sit against them cleanly; on Kits 01, 03 and 04 keep figures inside a white or light surface card rather than placing them on the page background.
  • Maximum two background tones per deck: the page background and one accent-filled divider or title slide.
