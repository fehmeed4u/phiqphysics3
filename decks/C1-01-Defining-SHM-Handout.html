WRAP-UPMark scheme [5]

✓ (a) The restoring force (and so the acceleration) is proportional to the displacement from equilibrium

✓ (a) and is always directed towards the equilibrium position: a = −ω²x

✓ (b) Doubling the amplitude doubles the displacement at every equivalent point

✓ (b) so the restoring force and the acceleration double, and the average speed doubles

✓ (b) the oscillator covers twice the distance at twice the average speed, so the time for one cycle is unchanged

Common zero-scoring answer: "because the period does not depend on amplitude" — a restatement of the question, with no chain of reasoning.
