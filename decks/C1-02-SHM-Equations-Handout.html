STARTERRetrieval — five questions on prior knowledge

1. Which statement defines simple harmonic motion?

ANS
  • AThe velocity is proportional to the displacement and in the same direction
  • BThe acceleration is constant and directed towards the equilibrium position
  • CThe acceleration is proportional to the displacement and in the opposite direction
  • DThe period is proportional to the amplitude of the oscillation

2. A mass on a spring oscillates with period T. The mass is replaced by one four times larger, on the same spring. The new period is

ANS
  • AT/2
  • B2T
  • C4T
  • D16T

3. A particle in SHM is at maximum displacement. At that instant

ANS
  • Avelocity and acceleration are both maximum
  • Bvelocity and acceleration are both zero
  • Cvelocity is maximum and acceleration is zero
  • Dvelocity is zero and acceleration is maximum

4. A simple pendulum and a mass–spring system are both taken to the Moon. Compared with on Earth, their periods

ANS
  • Apendulum increases, mass–spring unchanged
  • Bpendulum unchanged, mass–spring increases
  • Cboth increase
  • Dboth unchanged

5. The phase difference between the displacement and the acceleration of a particle in SHM is

ANS
  • A0
  • Bπ/2 rad
  • Cπ rad
  • D2π rad

CHECK 1Angular frequency and displacement

1. A particle undergoes SHM described by x = 0.05 cos(4πt). What is the angular frequency and period?

ANS
  • Aω = 4π rad s⁻¹, T = 4π s
  • Bω = 4π rad s⁻¹, T = 0.50 s
  • Cω = 2π rad s⁻¹, T = 1.0 s
  • Dω = 4π rad s⁻¹, T = 2.0 s

2. A particle undergoes SHM starting at its mean position (x = 0 at t = 0). Which equation correctly describes its displacement?

ANS
  • Ax = x₀ cos(ωt)
  • Bx = x₀ sin(ωt)
  • Cx = x₀ tan(ωt)
  • Dx = x₀ cos(ωt) + x₀

3. For a particle with x = x₀ cos(ωt), the displacement at t = 0 is

ANS
  • Azero
  • Bx₀
  • C−x₀
  • Dωx₀

4. A particle has x = 0.020 cos(10t). Its period is

ANS
  • A0.10 s
  • B0.63 s
  • C1.6 s
  • D0.31 s

CHECK 2Velocity, acceleration and phase

1. In SHM, when is the velocity zero?

ANS
  • AAt the mean position only
  • BWhen acceleration is zero
  • CAt the extreme positions (x = ±x₀)
  • DVelocity is never zero in SHM

2. The phase difference between velocity and acceleration in SHM is:

ANS
  • A0 (in phase)
  • Bπ/2 rad (quarter cycle)
  • Cπ rad (antiphase)
  • D2π rad (full cycle)

3. For x = x₀ sin(ωt), the velocity is

ANS
  • Av = ωx₀ sin(ωt)
  • Bv = −ωx₀ sin(ωt)
  • Cv = ωx₀ cos(ωt)
  • Dv = −ωx₀ cos(ωt)

4. A particle undergoes SHM with x = 0.10 sin(5.0t). What is its maximum speed?

ANS
  • A0.10 m s⁻¹
  • B0.50 m s⁻¹
  • C2.5 m s⁻¹
  • D5.0 m s⁻¹

CHECK 3Velocity without time

1. For a particle in SHM, which correctly gives the speed at x = x₀/2?

ANS
  • Av = ωx₀/2
  • Bv = ωx₀/4
  • Cv = ωx₀√3/2 ≈ 0.866 ωx₀
  • Dv = ωx₀√2/2 ≈ 0.707 ωx₀

2. The speed of a particle in SHM at the equilibrium position (x = 0) is

ANS
  • Aω²x₀
  • Bωx₀
  • Cx₀/ω
  • Dω/x₀

3. A particle oscillates in SHM with amplitude 0.040 m and frequency 3.0 Hz. Its maximum acceleration is closest to

ANS
  • A0.75 m s⁻²
  • B3.6 m s⁻²
  • C14 m s⁻²
  • D3.6 × 10² m s⁻²

4. A particle in SHM passes through x = +0.020 m twice in every cycle — once moving right, once moving left. Its speed at those two instants is

ANS
  • Adifferent, because velocity is a vector
  • Bthe same, since v = ±ω√(x₀² − x²) gives equal magnitude
  • Czero, since it's not at an extreme
  • Dundefined without knowing the initial phase

WRAP-UPExam-style question

A particle of mass 0.200 kg undergoes SHM with amplitude 0.060 m and period 0.80 s. (i) Calculate the angular frequency ω. (ii) Write an expression for the displacement as a function of time if the particle starts at x = +x₀ at t = 0. (iii) Calculate the maximum speed and the maximum acceleration. (iv) Calculate the speed when the displacement is 0.040 m.
