Grade 12 · Day 101
Before anything else

This year will feel like a lot. It is designed to be enough.

Deadlines will stack up — IA drafts, topic tests, trial exams. That's normal, and it's not a sign anything has gone wrong. It gets easier to manage with determination and resilience: keep showing up, keep submitting, keep going when a draft comes back with more red pen than you hoped for.

The one thing that matters most: stay on the deadline timeline. Don't let a submission slip because it feels easier to fix it "properly" later — a rough draft in on time beats a perfect one that's late. Once your IA and your mocks are done and out of the way, the rest of the year is just you and your exam papers — and that part is much easier to navigate.

Grade 12 · Day 103
The two dates that matter most right now

19 October and 9 November

First Submission — 19 October. This is the draft I give full written feedback on. It does not need to be finished — it needs to be in, with every section attempted, so the feedback has something to work with.

Final Submission — 9 November. This is the version that gets marked. Everything between the two dates is you responding to feedback, not starting over.

Everything else on the timeline — trials, topic tests, revision weeks — is easier to plan around once these two are locked in and treated as fixed.

Grade 12 · Day 104
What I expect from you this year

Four things — prepared, adaptable, reflective, kind

Prepared

Ready before the lesson starts, not scrambling during it.

Adaptable

Responds to feedback and setbacks by adjusting, not defending.

Reflective

Turns every result into a named lesson, not just a mark.

Kind

Looks out for classmates without being asked to.
